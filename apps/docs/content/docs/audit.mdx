---
title: "Audit trail"
description: "Connect user intent, risk decisions, signatures, responses, and fills."
---

```sh
agent-cli audit
agent-cli audit --intent YOUR_INTENT_ID
agent-cli audit --cloid 0x11111111111111111111111111111111
```

Trading actions accept `intentId` and `intent` (or `--intent-id` and `--intent`).
Use a stable `cloid` to reconcile retries. The journal at `~/.bloxwap/audit.jsonl`
records intent, decision, signature digest, response, and linked fill stages.
Entries include `seq`, UTC `timestamp`, `intentId`, optional `cloid` and `requestId`,
`stage`, redacted `data`, `previousHash`, and SHA-256 `hash`.

`audit` verifies the complete chain before filtering. Each entry hashes its payload
and references the previous hash; sequence numbers are checked. Appends use a
private lock and sync the file. Raw signatures are never recorded, only digests.

The chain detects edits to a retained journal. It cannot detect deletion of the
whole journal or replacement with a newly valid chain without an external
checkpoint. Back up checkpoints separately. Same-user malware and root can change
local files and configuration; see [Security](/docs/security).
